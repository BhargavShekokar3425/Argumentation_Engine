Key Theoretical Insights

1. Strict Sufficiency of Theorem 5.4: In all 3,000 generated instances across all edge densities, there was zero instance where Theorem 5.4 condition was met and the mechanism failed to be strategyproof. This confirms the mathematical correctness of Theorem 5.4.

2. Conservatism of the Condition: A substantial fraction of instances (898 instances total: 526 at p=0.15, 242 at p=0.25, 130 at p=0.35) are strategyproof even though Theorem 5.4 fails. This occurs when an agent owns indirect self-defeating arguments that are protected by other unattacked arguments or canceled out through conflicting attackers.

3. Density Effect: As attack probability p increases from 0.15 to 0.35, the graph becomes dense with cycles and attack paths, drastically reducing the proportion of strategyproof frameworks from 59.2% down to 13.0%.
